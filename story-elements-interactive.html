Rhea found an old, leather-bound diary in the attic. The cover was dusty, and the lock looked ancient. As she opened it, a faded photograph slipped out. It showed a girl standing by a mysterious door in a forest. Just then, she heard a soft noise coming from the stairs. She froze, wondering who—or what—was there.

Tip: Pay attention to the character, where events happen, what unfolds, the problem, and the bigger message.
